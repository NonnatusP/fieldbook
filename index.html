Install it once, then it opens like an app and works with no signal.

Sets prism ±(2 mm + 2 ppm), reflectorless ±(3 mm + 2 ppm) and a 2″ angle accuracy. The index limit (2″) and 2C limit (8″) are my assumptions scaled from that accuracy, not manufacturer figures.

Face difference allowed is √2 × (fixed part + ppm × distance in km). The triangle angle-sum limit is about 2√6 × angle accuracy. These are approximate 2σ figures, not a standard. Use your project specification for traverse limits.
